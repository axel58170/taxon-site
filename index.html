Common and scientific names

One name, many languages.

Enter a common or scientific name and see what that animal, plant, or fungus is called in all your selected languages.

See Taxon Translate in action

Look up a species in the app

Want to know what, say, a House Sparrow is called in, for example, French or Dutch? Enter any common or scientific name. Taxon Translate finds the matching animal, plant, or fungus and shows its names in the languages you selected.

Look up species from other apps

Using the Share Sheet

Choose Taxon Translate in the Share Sheet to:

  • look up a species while reading;
  • look up a bird from Merlin;
  • look up a species from Wikipedia; or
  • look up a species with Visual Intelligence after opening its Wikipedia page.

Taxon Translate shows the names in your selected languages in a pop-up over the other app.

Taxon Translate is not affiliated with or endorsed by Merlin Bird ID, eBird, or the Cornell Lab of Ornithology.

Using Shortcuts

To look up a species from Books, add the Lookup Species Shortcut once. Select and copy the species name, then run the Shortcut.

Add the Lookup Species Shortcut

  • Set up your languagesSelect the languages you want to see and put them in the order you prefer.
  • See the scientific nameThe scientific name is shown together with the translations.
  • Find your recent searches againSpecies you look up from another app also appear in Recent Searches in Taxon Translate. You can return to them later without looking them up again.
  • Use Shortcuts and SiriUse Shortcuts to look up a species, get a translation in one language, or show the translations in your selected languages. You can also run your Shortcuts with Siri.

The whole living world

More than birds

Taxon Translate is not limited to birds. A plant on a gardening website, a mushroom mentioned in a recipe, or an unfamiliar animal in an article can all be looked up in exactly the same way.
